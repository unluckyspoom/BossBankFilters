# Boss Bank Filter

A RuneLite plugin that remembers the loot you get from each boss or monster
and lets you filter your bank down to items from the sources you pick.

## How to use
1. Make sure the **Loot Tracker** and **Bank Tags** plugins are enabled.
2. Open the Boss Bank Filter panel from the sidebar and tick the sources you want.
3. Open your bank and click **Show in bank**. Click **Clear filter** to go back.

Only loot received while this plugin is on is recorded, and the filter only
shows items that are currently in your bank.
